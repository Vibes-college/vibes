# Vibes reference covers

Seven PNG covers are screenshots of the owner's existing Vibes editorial cover
art from https://vibes.college/zh/, captured on 2026-10-03 for this local demo.
The Chinese guides were imported from the owner's earlier Vibes
project. Each article retains its Vibes link and the original work's source.

The three feature-visualization WebP images are copied from Vibes's prepared
assets. Original: https://distill.pub/2017/feature-visualization/.
Creators: Chris Olah, Alexander Mordvintsev, Ludwig Schubert.
License: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
Changes: resizing and WebP conversion by Vibes. Credit is also in article captions.

External video/podcast pages remain source links. These previews do not copy or
rehost their audiovisual recordings.
